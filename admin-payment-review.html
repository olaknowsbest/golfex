Security

  • No seed phrase, private key, or other wallet recovery material is ever requested, exposed, or stored anywhere in this system.
  • No real wallet address, QR code, or usable transaction identifier appears anywhere in this static prototype.
  • Production Laravel loads the authoritative payment and order record from MySQL, never from anything the browser supplies — query-string values are never trusted for price or status.
