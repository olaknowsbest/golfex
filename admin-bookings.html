Availability guidance

  • Customer booking times are generated only from windows an administrator has actively published here — never from a free-form request.
  • A blocked period, or a period already reserved by another customer, is never selectable at checkout.
  • Checkout atomically holds the selected slot. A successful, manually verified payment confirms the booking; an expired or rejected payment releases the hold back to availability.

This screen manages availability windows only. No customer bookings, payments, or personal information appear anywhere on this page.
