How verification works

Payment is never approved automatically. After sending the exact quoted amount, you will submit the transaction hash for that payment. An administrator manually verifies the network, recipient address, amount, confirmations, transaction success, and that the transaction hash has not already been used, before the payment is approved.

Live payment quoting is unavailable.
