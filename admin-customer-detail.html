How account management will work

  • Customer identity and ownership always come from authenticated backend records, never from a query parameter or anything else the browser supplies.
  • Suspension blocks sign-in and new purchases but preserves records and full audit history. It never silently cancels an active Membership, invalidates a Fan Card, deletes a Donation, or erases a Booking — any entitlement or booking change requires its own explicit workflow.
  • Email address changes require re-verification. Account restoration is always explicit and audited.
  • Every admin action records the administrator, timestamp, reason, and affected customer.
  • Password hashes, authentication tokens, OAuth tokens, session identifiers, wallet details, private notes, and other sensitive security metadata are never exposed anywhere in this system.
  • Customer deletion requires a separate privacy and retention workflow and is intentionally absent from this prototype. No login-as-customer, impersonation, or password-reset action exists here either.
