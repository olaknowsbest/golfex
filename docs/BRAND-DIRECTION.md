# Nazario Kayla Prototype — Brand and Public Content Direction

## Placeholder identity boundary

This repository contains a platform prototype for the fictional placeholder athlete Nazario Kayla:

- Label development previews as `Prototype`.
- Do not imply that Nazario Kayla is a real athlete or connect the placeholder to a real person's identity.
- Do not accept real payments, bookings, donations, subscriptions, or personal-message requests until the production business, policies, backend, and operational controls are approved.
- Use original or appropriately licensed generic golf imagery, invented placeholder statistics, and clearly labeled demonstration content.
- Do not copy the visual identity or proprietary assets of the dashboard reference site; use its information architecture only as inspiration.

Any later replacement of the fictional identity with a real person requires separate authorization, asset rights, sponsorship review, privacy terms, consumer disclosures, and legal review before launch.

## Brand position

Desired feeling:

- World-class but approachable
- Athletic, precise, calm, and modern
- Premium without looking like a generic luxury or cryptocurrency site
- Golf-informed without repeating the original Golfex club template
- Community-led rather than celebrity-commerce-led

Recommended working name for internal design:

`Nazario Kayla Fan Club — Prototype`

Do not invent a management-company name or imply real-world representation.

## Visual system

Keep the approved Golfex foundation:

- Ink black: `#0A0A0A`
- Warm cream: `#FCFBF5`
- Soft sand: `#F9F7EA`
- Highlight yellow: `#FDF567`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted gray: use accessible values rather than the existing low-contrast gray everywhere
- Typography: Inter Tight

Public site:

- Cream editorial surfaces
- Black type
- Large golf/action photography when licensed
- Yellow used for focused calls to action and small graphic details

Future dashboard:

- Black/charcoal application shell
- Cream-white primary text
- Yellow active states and primary actions
- Muted sand cards or restrained yellow outlines for important information
- Status colors reserved for actual meaning: green success, amber review, red rejection, gray inactive

Avoid adopting the reference site's electric-blue identity. The public site and dashboard should feel like one brand.

## Logo direction suggestions

Approved original concept directions for the fictional placeholder identity:

1. **NK line monogram** — geometric `N` and `K` formed from two precise golf-swing arcs.
2. **Ball-flight mark** — minimal circle and rising trajectory paired with a restrained `NK` wordmark.
3. **Flag-and-initial mark** — abstract pin flag created from negative space between the initials.

Avoid crowns, shields, generic luxury crests, copied signatures, and marks resembling established sports, equipment, tour, team, or athlete branding.

## Public homepage outline

1. Hero: placeholder athlete name, prototype disclosure, working tagline, `Explore the Journey`, and `View Fan Cards`.
2. Career introduction: clearly labeled fictional placeholder biography.
3. Career highlights: invented demonstration statistics labeled as placeholders and stored as replaceable content.
4. Latest news and media: fictional demonstration cards or generic golf editorial placeholders.
5. Fan-card packages: Standard, Premium, VIP concept cards.
6. Fan experiences: request-based experiences with clear availability and approval language.
7. Partners/sponsors: show only with permission and current verification.
8. Community/testimonials: use real, consented testimonials only.
9. FAQ: explain concept status, fulfillment, payment review, booking approval, and support.
10. Footer: contact, privacy, terms, refund/cancellation policy, accessibility, and concept disclosure.

## Placeholder content rules

Until project-owned final content is supplied:

- Do not import a real athlete's biography, results, rankings, sponsors, equipment, quotes, or news.
- Label invented biography and statistics as placeholder/demo content in authoring notes.
- Keep content replaceable rather than embedding it throughout scripts or styles.
- Do not fabricate endorsements, charity relationships, awards, or customer testimonials.

## Photography plan

For design development, use one of these paths:

1. Owner supplies project-owned or licensed local assets with usage notes.
2. Use neutral golf photography with suitable commercial licenses.
3. Use clearly temporary geometric or photo placeholders.
4. Create original abstract golf visuals that do not depict a recognizable real athlete.

Do not download images from search results, social media, tours, teams, stock previews, sponsors, or news sites merely because they are publicly viewable.
